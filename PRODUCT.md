# Product

## Register

product

## Users

BlakSync serves staff and members of Australian Indigenous organisations who
need to share working files between office, field and community-controlled
devices. Users may be non-technical, work in regional locations with uneven
connectivity and need to understand exactly which device can access each
folder.

## Product Purpose

Provide calm, local-first folder sync without a vendor-controlled cloud copy.
Success means people can pair devices, read cultural access notes, accept or
deny shares and confirm sync health without needing to understand Syncthing's
internal terminology.

## Brand Personality

Grounded, capable and respectful. BlakSync should feel community-controlled,
plain-spoken and dependable, with the warmth of the Australian landscape and
the clarity of a trusted work tool.

## Anti-references

Avoid cold generic cloud-drive styling, neon developer dashboards, decorative
SaaS conventions, tourist-outback kitsch, sacred or ceremonial motifs and
pseudo-Indigenous patterning. Do not imply that one visual language represents
all Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander communities.

## Design Principles

- Keep local and community control visible in the product chrome and copy.
- Make device trust, folder access and sync state obvious without relying on
  colour alone.
- Draw warmth from landscape colour and material restraint, never imitation of
  cultural artwork.
- Make the next safe action obvious to a non-technical user.
- Prefer familiar, keyboard-operable controls that remain clear on small and
  low-resolution screens.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA. Support keyboard and screen-reader use, visible focus, text
scaling, reduced motion, increased contrast and status communication that pairs
colour with explicit text. Use plain Australian English and preserve useful
layouts from 320px wide upward.
