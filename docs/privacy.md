# Privacy notice

Australian English. This is user-facing copy, not a legal review.

BlakSync does not store your file bytes. Copies live on the devices your organisation already owns. Yuma does not run a cloud drive for this product.

Global discovery and volunteer relays can see device IDs and roughly where a device is reachable. That metadata is not a secret from those services. Turn them off under Settings if you only need the local network or Tailscale.

There is no social login and no government ID. Unshare and revoke stop future copies. Files already on a disk stay there. We cannot wipe a lost laptop.

For help, email hello@yumait.com.au or the contact on this organisation's profile.
